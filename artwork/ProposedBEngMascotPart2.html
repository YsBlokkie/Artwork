Hammer Head: Mascot Proposal

Medium

Pencil and pen concept sheet

Timing & Occasion

27 May 2022, a companion piece to the Nikola mascot proposal, from the same branding project.

Description

27 May 2022, A second alternative mascot concept from the same project (see Nikola the Lightbulb). A hammerhead inspired bird character, worked through a range of poses. It wasn't submitted for the competition, just an idea that I came up with on my own. Presenting two very different concepts side by side, a lightbulb inventor versus a hammerhead bird, I like to imagine that the two of them would be friends and co-mascots together.
