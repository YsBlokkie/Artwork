To Your Valour

Medium

Pen and coloured pencil

Timing & Occasion

23 June 2024, a drawing made for fun.

Description

A chibi tribute to the Dark Souls III game. It shows my avatar during my first playthrough and the character, Siegward of Catarina, slumped together sharing a toast after our first battle victory together. A lighthearted and cheerful moment in this otherwise dark and grim game.
