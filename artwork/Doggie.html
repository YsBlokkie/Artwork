Just a Bird

Medium

Pen illustration

Timing & Occasion

28 March 2025, an unfinished sketch made for fun.

Description

Some time in December 2024 near Christmas day, we discovered a poor lost chick in our backyard. We took it into our care and it quickly became part of our household as a pet. As it matured, we came to realize that it was a Cape Spurfowl, indigenous and endemic to southern Africa. These sketches showcase his daily behaviour and take heavy inspiration from HeiHei, the rooster character from the Disney movie Moana.
