Plastic Ocean: Concept Sketches

Medium

Pencil sketch with pen outlining

Timing & Occasion

Early brainstorming from 2021, done ahead of the Matrics in Antarctica competition.

Description

Three unfinished sketches were drawn in the process of figuring out how showcase the difference between plastic vs glass in how it impacts the environment. Octopi typically love to crawl inside closed spaces, so I figured that the animal that should take the center stage to show the diffrence, would be an octopus. Glass items like bottles and jars hold their shape under water pressure and weight over plastic items that crumble and float around. Glass therefore provides a stable, cave-like roof that mimics natural dens. Unlike plastic that collapses or remains too flexible to offer real physical protection for these critters. This is the seed that grew into the finished "Plastic Kills, Use Glass" piece.
