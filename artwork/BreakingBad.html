Say Their Name

Medium

Pen illustration

Timing & Occasion

18 July 2024: Fan-art made for fun.

Description

I started watching one of the most groundbreaking and defining shows in television history known as "Breaking Bad" in 2024. After completing the entire show, I can understand why it became so popular and it stands high on my list of favorite shows. In tribute to it, I redrew a scene from a funny commercial of the show in a manga-like art style. If Naoki Urasawa were ever to create a manga based on Breaking Bad, I would one hundred percent read it.
