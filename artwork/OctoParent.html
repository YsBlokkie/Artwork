Octo Love

Medium

Orange ink illustration

Timing & Occasion

19 June 2023, A personal piece about comfort and care.

Description

A tender, minimal sketch of an octopus-headed parent cradling a sleepy octopus child against their shoulder. Octopi are not known for exhibiting nurturing maternal behaviors, but this piece is an alternative take on their biology.
