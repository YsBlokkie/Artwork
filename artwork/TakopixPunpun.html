Comfort Toys

Medium

Pen illustration

Timing & Occasion

20 December 2025, drawn in response to similarities in both stories.

Description

Two girls stand side by side, each hugging a plush toy. One modelled on Takopi from Takopi's Original Sin and the other on the bird-headed lead of Goodnight Punpun. Their withdrawn expressions echo both stories' themes of childhood loneliness.
