Mind If I Join You?

Medium

Pen illustration, three-panel comic

Timing & Occasion

29 June 2025, a personal mood piece.

Description

A quiet three-panel comic pairing characters from Goodnight Punpun and Omori, two stories that both deal openly with isolation and mental health. The second panel is printed and meant to represent Punpun's dialogue box, which is used often within the Goodnight Punpun manga.
