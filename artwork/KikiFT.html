Kiki the Exceed

Medium

Pen and coloured pencil

Timing & Occasion

15 October 2024, an affectionate, personal piece drawn for fun.

Description

A pet portrait sheet depicting our late pug, Kiki as an exceed companion from the Fairy Tail anime. Complete with wings, teary excitement, a happy snack moment and a cute husky friend. This was made as a keepsake to remember a beloved member of our family.
