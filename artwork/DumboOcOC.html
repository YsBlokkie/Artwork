Dumbo Octopus Character Sheet

Medium

Pen and coloured pencil

Timing & Occasion

06 September 2022, a thorough sheet for developing a recurring original character.

Description

A full expression and situation sheet for an original character based on the Grimpoteuthis octopod species, one of my favorite animals. Many scenarios illustrated to lock down on how the character could possibly behave and react. It now sits as a reference for future drawings of the same character.
