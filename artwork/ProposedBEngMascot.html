Nikola the Lightbulb: Mascot Proposal

Medium

Pencil and pen, concept sheet

Timing & Occasion

27 May 2022, Developed as a pitch for Stellenbosch's engineering faculty project.

Description

A mascot design proposal: A lightbulb-headed figure named Nikola, a nod to the famous inventor, Nikola Tesla. The idea was developed with a fellow classmate at the time, and we both submitted the concept art for the competition that the faculty held. It unfortunatley didn't win the competition, regardless it was fun developing the character and brainstorming ideas together.
