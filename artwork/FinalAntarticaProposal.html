Plastic Kills, Use Glass

Medium

Pen illustration

Timing & Occasion

17 November 2021, submitted as the final entry for the 2021 Matrics in Antarctica competition.

Description

A crowded underwater scene, depicting the horrors of plastic pollution and the destructive impact it has on the lifeforms living in the ocean. This was the finished submission for the 2021 Matrics in Antarctica competition, paired with a video presentation on choosing sustainable packaging over plastic. Unfortunately, I only managed to reach the Top 50 during this competition, but it was still a fun experience and probably one of the first times that I made my art public online. For more information, check out their website at https://www.matricsinantarctica.co.za
