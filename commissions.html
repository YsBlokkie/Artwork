Commission Information

Thank you for considering commissioning artwork from me. Every piece is created by hand using traditional materials and gets my full attention from the first sketch to the final line.

How It Works

  1. Contact me by email with your idea, references and any important details.
  2. We talk through the concept, size, medium and any specific requests.
  3. You'll get a price estimate based on the complexity of the piece.
  4. Work begins once we've agreed on the details.
  5. I share progress photos throughout so you can follow the piece taking shape.
  6. Feedback is welcome at every stage to make sure the final piece is right.
  7. The finished artwork is delivered once the commission is complete.

Pricing

Prices are worked out individually and depend on:

  • Artwork size
  • Level of detail
  • Medium used
  • Number of characters
  • Background complexity
  • Estimated completion time

Good to Know

  • Traditional artwork only, no digital pieces.
  • Anime, cartoon and character-focused commissions are especially welcome.
  • Clear communication and transparency are a priority throughout.
  • Services are currently available only within South Africa.
